Harmonize your scalp health and amplify natural shine. The LIVA Hair Ritual Duo combines our gentle balancing cleanse with our peptide-rich conditioning therapy for resilient, flowing, silky-smooth hair.

How to Use & Application Ritual

For maximum botanical bio-availability, integrate LIVA Hair Ritual Duo into your daily self-care ritual following our dermatologist-developed 3-step application method:

STEP 01
Dispense & Warm

Dispense an appropriate amount into the palm of clean hands. Gently warm between palms for 3 seconds to activate delicate botanical plant essences.

STEP 02
Mindful Application

Gently massage onto damp skin or hair using smooth, upward circular motions. Breathe in the natural aromatherapeutic notes of bergamot, rosemary, and sage.

STEP 03
Absorb & Lock In

Allow active plant ceramides to absorb for 2–3 minutes before rinsing thoroughly with lukewarm water, or leave on as a silky botanical protective barrier.

Botanical Care Note: Safe for daily morning and evening use. Formulated for all skin and hair types including sensitive skin. Store in a cool, dry area away from direct sunlight.
