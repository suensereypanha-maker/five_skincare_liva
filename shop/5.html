Botanical Product Overview

Provide 24-hour hydration without greasy residue. LIVA Hydrating Body Lotion melts effortlessly upon contact, locking in moisture with olive squalane and calming irritated or dry skin with antioxidant-rich green tea leaf extracts.

Key Benefits & Highlights
  • Fast-absorbing velvet finish with zero sticky residue
  • Phytosqualane and sweet almond oil reinforce moisture seal
  • Calms dry redness and restores silky skin elasticity
  • Dermatologically tested for sensitive skin
How to Use & Application Ritual

For maximum botanical bio-availability, integrate LIVA Hydrating Body Lotion into your daily self-care ritual following our dermatologist-developed 3-step application method:

STEP 01
Dispense & Warm

Dispense an appropriate amount into the palm of clean hands. Gently warm between palms for 3 seconds to activate delicate botanical plant essences.

STEP 02
Mindful Application

Gently massage onto damp skin or hair using smooth, upward circular motions. Breathe in the natural aromatherapeutic notes of bergamot, rosemary, and sage.

STEP 03
Absorb & Lock In

Allow active plant ceramides to absorb for 2–3 minutes before rinsing thoroughly with lukewarm water, or leave on as a silky botanical protective barrier.

Botanical Care Note: Safe for daily morning and evening use. Formulated for all skin and hair types including sensitive skin. Store in a cool, dry area away from direct sunlight.
